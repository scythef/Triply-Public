Short version: PupanCo is designed to help people share their live location only within a trip or activity group. PupanCo does not require an account, does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, or payment information, and does not sell user data.

1. About PupanCo

PupanCo is a privacy-first group location sharing application for trips, outdoor activities, events, and similar group coordination scenarios. PupanCo allows members of the same trip to see each other on a map while the trip is active.

2. Information PupanCo Collects

PupanCo collects only the information needed to provide the app's core functionality:

  • Location data: precise location while a user participates in a trip and chooses to share location.
  • Trip information: trip identifiers, trip membership, invite codes or links, and basic trip state.
  • Anonymous device or app identifier: a randomly generated identifier used to operate the service without requiring a user account.
  • Operational data: basic technical logs needed to operate, secure, debug, and improve reliability of the service.

3. Information PupanCo Does Not Intentionally Collect

PupanCo is designed to avoid unnecessary personal data collection. PupanCo does not intentionally collect:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Address book or contacts
  • Photos or media files
  • Payment information
  • Advertising identifiers for advertising or profiling

4. How PupanCo Uses Information

PupanCo uses collected information to:

  • Show trip members on a live map.
  • Allow users to create, join, leave, and manage trips.
  • Keep trip location sharing working in real time.
  • Protect the service from misuse and technical failures.
  • Diagnose bugs and maintain app reliability.

5. Location Data

Location sharing is the core functionality of PupanCo. When location sharing is active, location data is used to show the user's position to other members of the same trip. Location data is not intended to be public and is not intended to be shown to people outside the trip.

Users should only join trips with people they trust. Anyone with access to a valid trip invite may be able to join the trip depending on the current app functionality.

6. Sharing of Information

PupanCo does not sell user data. PupanCo does not share user data with advertising networks or data brokers.

PupanCo may use cloud infrastructure providers, such as Microsoft Azure, to host and operate the service. These providers process data only as needed to provide infrastructure, storage, security, and operational services for PupanCo.

7. Data Retention

PupanCo keeps trip and location data only for as long as reasonably needed to operate the service, maintain reliability, troubleshoot issues, and support active or recent trips. PupanCo may delete inactive trip data as part of normal service maintenance.

If a user leaves a trip, the user should no longer be shown as an active participant in that trip. Some technical records may remain for a limited period for security, reliability, or debugging purposes.

8. User Choices and Deletion

Users can stop sharing location by leaving a trip, disabling location permission for PupanCo in device settings, or uninstalling the app.

To request deletion of data associated with PupanCo, contact rehoricsapps@gmail.com. Because PupanCo does not require accounts or collect names or email addresses, PupanCo may need information such as a trip identifier or device-generated identifier to locate relevant data.

9. Security

PupanCo uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No online service can guarantee perfect security.

10. Children's Privacy

PupanCo is not directed to children. Users should only use PupanCo if they are old enough to consent to location sharing under applicable laws or have appropriate permission from a parent or guardian.

11. International Processing

PupanCo may process information using cloud infrastructure located in countries different from the user's country. Where required, PupanCo will handle personal data in accordance with applicable privacy and data protection laws.

12. Changes to This Policy

PupanCo may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact rehoricsapps@gmail.com.
