Frequently Asked Questions

What is Triply?

Triply is a group location sharing app for trips, activities, and events. It helps members of the same trip see each other on a map.

Do I need an account?

Triply is designed to work without requiring a traditional account such as name, email address, or phone number.

Who can see my location?

Your location is intended to be visible only to members of the same trip while location sharing is active.

How do I stop sharing my location?

You can stop sharing by leaving the trip, disabling location permission for Triply in your device settings, or uninstalling the app.

Why is my location not updating?

Check that location permission is enabled for Triply, the device has network connectivity, battery saver is not blocking background activity, and the trip is still active.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Send a deletion request to rehoricsapps@gmail.com. Because Triply avoids collecting names and email addresses, include the relevant trip identifier or device-generated identifier if available.

Privacy and Safety

Only join trips with people you trust. Do not share invite links publicly unless you want others to potentially access the trip. Do not use Triply to track or monitor anyone without appropriate consent.
